2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to operate recovery features (streaks, journals, SOS, AI counseling). We do not sell personal data, show ads, or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and secure database storage.
  • Google Sign-In — optional login (subject to Google's Privacy Policy).
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini) — processes AI counselor messages.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. Use Settings → Delete All Data to wipe your journal, streak, and cloud SOS logs instantly — this is irreversible. Signed-in users may also request deletion by emailing soni.110051@gmail.com from their account email; requests are processed within 7 business days.

5. Security

Data in transit uses industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable safeguards.

6. Children

Mind is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please use the app only with a parent or guardian.

7. Health Disclaimer

Mind is a self-help companion, not a medical device, and provides no medical diagnosis or treatment. Nothing here replaces professional medical or psychological help. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
